What I work on

  • Industrial image classification

    Classifiers that find defects in manufacturing images and keep up with line speed.

  • Models that hold up under domain shift

    Ensembles and transfer learning that keep a model accurate when products, lines or imaging conditions change.

  • Specification-aware inspection

    Judging each defect against the customer's own specification, because the same defect can pass for one customer and fail for another.

  • Selective LLM inference

    Cascades that call a language model only for ambiguous or high-risk samples, so cost and latency stay low.
